Our Comprehensive Benefits Package

🏥 Health Insurance

Comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage for you and your family. We cover 80% of premiums.

💰 Retirement Plans

🌴 Paid Time Off

25 days of PTO annually + 12 paid holidays. Unlimited sick leave for your well-being.

📚 Learning & Development

$2,000 annual budget for courses, conferences, certifications, and professional memberships.

🏋️ Wellness Program

Gym membership reimbursement ($50/month), mental health support, and wellness workshops.

Flexible remote and hybrid work arrangements. Home office setup stipend provided.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do benefits become effective?

Health insurance and most benefits begin on your first day of employment. Retirement plans are eligible after 30 days.

Can I add family members to my health insurance?

Yes, you can add your spouse/partner and dependent children to your medical, dental, and vision coverage.

How does the tuition reimbursement work?

Submit your course details for approval, complete the course with a passing grade, and receive reimbursement within 30 days.

What is the remote work policy?

We offer flexible hybrid arrangements. Most teams come to the office 2-3 days per week, with fully remote options available.

How do I access my benefits?

All benefits are managed through our HR portal. You'll receive login credentials during onboarding.
